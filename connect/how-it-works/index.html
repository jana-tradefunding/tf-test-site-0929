Four steps, start to settlement

01

You register and get your code

Registration takes around five minutes. We brand it with your business identity and place it on your quote and invoice templates.

02

Your customer scans it on their quote

Instead of one finance option or none, they see qualified options from 100+ products across our panel of 70+ lenders - non-intrusive, and entirely their choice.

03

The lender takes on the finance

The finance sits with the lender, not your business. No recourse, no chargeback exposure, no receivables on your books.

04

You get paid out

You're paid in full at settlement, the same as any standard invoice. The sale moves forward instead of stalling on a financing question.
