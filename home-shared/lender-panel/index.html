Our Lender Panel

A panel of 70+ lenders, across four categories.

No lender gets pushed because it pays us more. Cashper checks your profile across every category below and ranks what actually qualifies, on S.T.A.R. scoring.

Major Banks

The big four, and the balance-sheet strength that comes with them

Sharp pricing on secured funding for businesses with strong financials and full disclosure. Usually the slowest to approve, often the cheapest once approved.

Non-Bank Lenders

Purpose-built for speed and flexibility

Typically faster credit decisions, more flexible security requirements, and policies built around cash flow rather than just balance-sheet strength. Often the right fit when time matters.

Private Credit

For the deals the mainstream panel won't touch

Bespoke, larger, or higher-complexity facilities - construction, development, and situations where a standard credit box doesn't fit the deal.

Specialist Financiers

Deep expertise in one asset class or facility type

Equipment, invoice and trade finance specialists who know the asset or the transaction inside out.

We name categories rather than pairing individual lenders with indicative rates here - every real rate and score in your Compare Report is generated against your own profile, not a generic example.
