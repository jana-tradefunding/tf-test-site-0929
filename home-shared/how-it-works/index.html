Who (or what) is Cashper?

I'm Cashper, Trade Funding's resident ghost and voice of transparency. No tricks, no gimmicks, just straight answers about business finance.

The team chose me because finance can feel complicated, confusing, and, let's be honest, a little intimidating. My job is to make it clearer, simpler, and make sure you see through the noise to get the funding that actually fits your business.

Under the hood

Behind the friendly face, I'm an AI-powered matching engine.

I build your Compare Report from your lending profile, our panel of 70+ lenders and each lender's own criteria, then rank every eligible option on S.T.A.R. scoring, so the order isn't a guess.

S

Security

What the lender can claim if repayments stop, and how that compares with the alternatives.

T

Term

How long you're committed to it, and whether that matches what you actually need it for.

A

Amount

Whether the funding covers what you asked for, or leaves a gap you'd have to fill elsewhere.

R

Rate

The real cost, on a like-for-like basis with every other option in your report.

Everything you didn't know you needed to ask me

Why a ghost?

Because finance shouldn't be scary. Trade Funding believes business owners should know exactly what they're signing up for - no mystery, no confusion, just clear comparisons and real choices.

Are you a lender?

Nope. Lenders provide businesses with the funding they need, but not every option is the right fit for everyone. Trade Funding exists to help businesses compare their options confidently so they can choose the right lender for their needs.

What do you actually do?

I make sure Trade Funding sticks to its promise of clarity, honesty, and no tricks. I don't push products or play favourites. Behind the scenes, I'm also the engine that qualifies your business across our panel of 70+ lenders and generates your S.T.A.R.-scored Compare Report.

What's your deal with transparency?

It's kind of my thing. You can't get much more transparent than a ghost. Business owners deserve clear finance options, no hidden fees, and straight-up answers. If something doesn't add up, I call it out.

Can I trust you?

Well, I can't hold a pen, so I'm definitely not signing you up for anything. Trade Funding doesn't do pushy sales tactics, and neither do I. You get the facts, you make the decisions. Simple.

Do you have a last name?

Nope. Just Cashper. Like Beyoncé.
