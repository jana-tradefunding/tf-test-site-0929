Glossary

S.T.A.R. Scoring
Cashper's ranking framework: Security, Term, Amount, Rate. Every eligible offer in your Compare Report is scored against all four.
ABN
Australian Business Number - the first thing Cashper checks when matching your business across the lender panel.
Chattel Mortgage
You own the asset from day one while the lender holds a mortgage over it as security - common for vehicles and equipment.
Line of Credit
A revolving limit you draw on as needed, paying interest only on the amount actually drawn.
Invoice Finance
Releasing the cash tied up in an unpaid invoice instead of waiting the full payment term.
PPSR
Personal Property Securities Register - where a lender's security interest over an asset (like equipment) is formally registered.

General FAQs

What is a Compare Report?

A ranked, S.T.A.R.-scored list of the funding options your business, or you personally, actually qualify for, generated by checking your profile across our panel of 70+ lenders.

Do you charge fees to businesses?

Getting a Compare Report is free, with no credit score impact and no commitment. Connect is free to vendors too. Any fees on a specific product come from the lender, not us, and are disclosed before you apply.

Are you a lender?

No. We're a licensed credit intermediary (ACL 387856) who compares across a panel of lenders and supports you through to settlement.

Where do I find Connect-specific or Personal & Property FAQs?

See the Connect FAQs for vendor/customer questions, or get in touch via Contact for anything Personal & Property specific.

Know before you apply. We compare 100+ products across our panel of 70+ lenders, lay out your qualified options, then support you through to settlement. Free, with no credit score impact.
