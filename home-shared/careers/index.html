Cashper does the matching. Our people make the difference.

A culture built on transparency, innovation and collaboration, where we celebrate team and client success. A supportive, inclusive workplace where everyone can grow, contribute and build their skills. If you want to make a difference at a company driven by strong values, explore a career at Trade Funding.

Our Values

Total Transparency
No Smoke, No Mirrors

What you see is what you get. No hidden agendas, just straight talk and real relationships. We don't push what's most profitable; we show the possibilities and foster good decisions. We have the tough conversations, speak our minds and keep things real.

Move Fast
Speed Wins, Execution Matters

Business waits for no one. We execute and deliver on our promises without delay. Whether it's funding, solutions or support, we move with urgency, because timing can make or break opportunities.

As One
Your Success is Our Success

Every day is Day One, with the same hunger and drive. We operate as an extension of our clients' businesses, so they never feel like they're going it alone. Internally, we're one team, united by shared values, common goals and a commitment to the right outcome.

Keep It Simple
Clarity in Complexity

Finance, like a career decision, can be complicated, but it doesn't need to be. No industry jargon, no endless loops of explanation, just clear insights that help people decide with confidence. Light, approachable and judgment-free, for clients, partners and our own team.
