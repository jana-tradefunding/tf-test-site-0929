Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 September 2026

Intro Collection Use Disclosure Security Cookies Cross-Border Access Complaints Changes Contact

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Trade Funding Pty Ltd collects, holds, uses, and discloses your Personal Information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). By using our website or submitting an enquiry or application, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described below.

We / Us / OurTrade Funding Pty Ltd (ABN 28 647 543 084), Australian Credit Licence 387856
Personal InformationInformation or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable
Credit InformationInformation collected in connection with a credit application or account, including repayment history and default information, as defined under the Privacy Act
Product ProviderAny bank, non-bank lender, or credit provider we may introduce or refer you to
Vendor PartnerAny business registered with Trade Funding Connect to make point-of-sale finance available to its customers

We do not ordinarily collect Sensitive Information (health, racial or ethnic origin, criminal record) unless required to verify your identity or comply with a legal obligation, in which case we will seek your explicit consent.

2. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect Personal Information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services and to introduce you to suitable Product Providers, including:

Personal and contact details - name, date of birth, address, email, phone, and identity documents
Business information - ABN/ACN, trading name, structure, industry, and time in business
Financial information - revenue, bank statements, tax returns, and existing loans, assets, and liabilities
Credit information - credit reports, repayment history, enquiries, defaults, and court judgements
Website interaction data - IP address, device and browser information, and pages visited

We collect this directly from you wherever practicable - for example, through an online enquiry form, a call, or an email. We may also collect information from third parties such as credit reporting bodies and identity verification services where permitted by law.

3. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use your Personal Information only for the purposes for which it was collected, or as otherwise permitted by the APPs, including to:

Verify your identity and administer our engagement with you
Assess your financing needs and prepare a personalised Compare Report
Submit applications or referrals to Product Providers on your behalf
Conduct or facilitate credit checks with credit reporting bodies
Send you service updates or educational content where you've consented - you may opt out at any time
Comply with the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth), the AML/CTF Act 2006 (Cth), and other applicable laws
Analyse website usage and improve our services

4. Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose your Personal Information to the following categories of third parties, to the extent necessary to provide our services or comply with our legal obligations:

Product Providers - banks, non-bank lenders, and other credit providers on our panel
Vendor Partners - where you arrived at us via a registered Vendor Partner, to confirm the underlying transaction and arrange settlement
Guarantors and related parties - where a guarantor is required as part of a credit application
Identity verification providers - to meet our AML/CTF obligations
Contractors and service providers - technology, IT, marketing, and professional services engaged to support our operations
Zoho - our customer relationship management system. Details you submit through our website forms go straight into it, so our team can respond and prepare your Compare Report
Vercel - hosts our website and passes form submissions to Zoho
Google Fonts - serves the typeface our website uses, and receives your IP address when a page loads
Australian Business Register - when you look up your business on our application form, the ABN or business name you type is sent from your browser to the ABR
Regulatory and government bodies - ASIC, AUSTRAC, the ATO, and others where disclosure is required or authorised by law
Credit reporting bodies - Equifax, Illion, Experian, and similar bodies as permitted under the Privacy Act

We will not sell, rent, or trade your Personal Information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Security of Your Information

We take reasonable steps - physical, electronic, and procedural - to protect your Personal Information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. Our systems are protected by SSL/TLS encryption, and access is restricted to authorised personnel on a need-to-know basis.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to us electronically. If you believe your interaction with us is no longer secure, contact us immediately.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website does not currently set cookies, and it runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Analytics - if we add a tool such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, it will only run after you agree to it
Preferences - if we ask for that agreement, we will store your choice so we don't ask again on every visit

Third-party services we use may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. You can disable or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

7. Cross-Border Disclosure

Some of our third-party service providers and technology infrastructure are located outside Australia. As a result, your Personal Information may be stored on, or accessible from, servers located in the United States and the Philippines, and other jurisdictions from time to time.

Zoho stores the details you submit through our website in its Australian data centre. Vercel, a US company, serves our website and processes form submissions through its global network, including servers in the United States and Singapore. Google Fonts is provided from Google's servers, including in the United States.

Where we disclose Personal Information to overseas recipients, we take reasonable steps to ensure they do not breach the APPs in relation to that information. By using our website, you consent to your information being held and processed in these jurisdictions.

8. Access and Correction

You have the right to request access to the Personal Information we hold about you, and to request correction of any information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. Contact us in writing using the details in Section 11 - we will respond within a reasonable timeframe, and in any event within 30 days.

In some circumstances we may decline a request where permitted by the APPs - for example, where it would unreasonably impact another individual's privacy. If we decline, we will provide written reasons and the steps you may take to escalate the matter.

9. Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy or handled your Personal Information inconsistently with this Policy or the APPs, contact us in the first instance so we can attempt to resolve your concern promptly. We will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days and aim to respond substantively within 30 days.
