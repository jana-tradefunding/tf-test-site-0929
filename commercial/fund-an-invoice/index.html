Frequently asked questions.

How does selective invoice finance work?

You choose a single invoice to fund on demand and upload it to a finance provider. They verify the debtor and advance you 70 to 90% of the invoice value, typically within 24 hours. When your customer pays, you receive the remaining balance minus the provider's fee. There's no requirement to fund your entire ledger or enter a long-term commitment.

How is Fund an Invoice different from the Invoice Finance facility?

Invoice Finance is an ongoing facility where you assign most or all of your invoices to a lender in exchange for a lower, volume-based fee. Fund an Invoice is a one-off, ad hoc alternative: you choose a single invoice, fund it once, and walk away with no ongoing commitment. It suits businesses with an occasional large invoice or a seasonal cash flow gap rather than a business wanting to fund its whole ledger every month. Because there's no volume commitment, the per-invoice cost is generally higher than a full facility.

How much does it cost to fund a single invoice?

Fees typically range from 1% to 4% of the invoice value per 30 days, depending on the lender, invoice size, debtor quality, and payment terms. Because there's no whole-ledger commitment, per-invoice costs are generally higher than full factoring or invoice discounting facilities.

What is the minimum invoice size?

Most selective invoice finance providers have a minimum invoice size of $5,000, though some may go as low as $1,000 or require a minimum of $10,000. The invoice must be a B2B invoice for completed work or delivered goods, and the debtor must be creditworthy.

What invoices qualify?

To qualify, invoices must be B2B, for completed work or delivered goods, not disputed or subject to offset, and issued to a creditworthy debtor. Progress claims, milestone invoices, and invoices with retentions may not be eligible. Your business typically needs at least 3 months of trading history.
