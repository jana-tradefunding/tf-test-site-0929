S

Security

Security (what the lender can claim if you don't repay) is usually the goods and their trade documents (letters of credit, bills of lading, invoices), plus a director's personal guarantee (personal liability). Larger facilities may also take business assets or property.

T

Term

Each drawdown (each use of the facility) runs for a 30 to 180 day trade cycle tied to a specific purchase order or shipment. Facilities are typically revolving, so a new cycle can start as soon as the last one settles.

A

Amount

Facilities range from $50,000 to $10,000,000+, sized against the value of your confirmed purchase orders and your trading history rather than a fixed borrowing limit.

R

Rate

Rates run from around 1.5% p.a. for major bank trade facilities to 8% p.a. for fast, specialist trade lenders, with letters of credit typically adding a further 0.5 to 2% of the letter of credit value. Your report shows the real number for each.

Frequently asked questions.

What exactly is trade finance and how does it work?

Trade finance is a broad term covering financial products that facilitate international and domestic trade. In its simplest form, a trade finance facility pays your suppliers on your behalf, and you repay the lender after an agreed period, typically 30 to 180 days. This means you can receive and sell goods before you need to pay for them, preserving your working capital.

How is trade finance different from invoice finance?

Trade finance funds the gap before goods are purchased: it pays your supplier so stock or materials can be shipped. Invoice finance funds the gap after a sale, unlocking cash tied up in an invoice you've already issued to your customer. They can be used together across the same trade cycle, but they solve different ends of the problem.

What security do trade finance lenders require?

Trade finance is often secured by the goods being traded, with the supporting trade documents (letters of credit, bills of lading, invoices) forming part of the security. Some lenders also require a General Security Agreement over your business assets, a personal guarantee from directors, or in some cases property security for larger facilities.

How long does it take to set up a trade finance facility?

Initial setup typically takes 7 to 21 business days from application to approval, depending on the lender and complexity of your trade. Major banks tend to take 14 to 21 days, while non-bank lenders can often approve in 7 to 14 days. Once a facility is established, individual drawdowns are typically processed within 1 to 3 business days.
