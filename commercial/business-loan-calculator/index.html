Calculator FAQs

How are business loan repayments calculated?

Repayments are calculated using a standard amortisation formula based on your loan amount, annual interest rate, and term. Each repayment includes both interest and a portion of the principal, structured so the loan is fully repaid by the end of the term.

What factors affect my business loan repayment amount?

The loan amount, interest rate, and term length are the main drivers. Your actual rate is also shaped by factors this calculator can't see, including security offered, trading history, and credit profile.

Should I choose weekly, fortnightly, or monthly repayments?

It depends on your cash flow. Weekly suits businesses with frequent revenue cycles, fortnightly aligns with standard payroll, and monthly is simplest for accounting and reconciliation.

Can I pay off my business loan early without a penalty?

This varies by lender and product. Some facilities allow early repayment with no penalty, others apply a break fee or require the remaining interest to be paid out. Confirm the terms with your specific lender before signing.
