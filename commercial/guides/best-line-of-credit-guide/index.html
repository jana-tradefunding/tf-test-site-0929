KEY TAKEAWAYS
No single "best" line of credit. The right one depends on limit size, how fast you need funds, and what security (assets the lender can claim if repayments stop) you can offer.

How "Best" Is Ranked

"Best" is doing a lot of work in a headline like this, so here's exactly what we're ranking on. A line of credit is a revolving facility - you're approved for a limit, draw against it as needed, and pay interest only on the drawn balance. That structure means the right choice isn't just "lowest rate." A facility with a brilliant rate that takes six weeks to set up is useless if you need funds inside a fortnight.

We rank across five criteria, in this order of practical importance for most Trade Funding clients: limit range (can you actually get the size you need), approval speed (how fast from application to funds available), security required (what you have to put up to qualify), drawdown flexibility (how easily you can pull funds once approved), and ongoing fees (line fees, redraw fees, and unused-limit charges that erode the headline rate).

Best For: Matching the Tier to the Job

The "best" line of credit changes depending on what you're actually optimising for. Four common scenarios, and which tier wins each one:

Best For: Fastest Approval
Fintech lenders win outright - typically 24 to 48 hours from application to a drawable limit, entirely online, no property valuation to wait on.
Best For: Lowest Rate
Big 4 Banks win on headline rate, especially with property or a general security agreement (GSA, a claim over the business's assets) backing the facility - but budget 2 to 4 weeks to get there.
Best For: No Security Required
Fintech lenders again - smaller limits approved against cash flow and a director guarantee alone (the director personally backs the debt). No GSA or property caveat.
Best For: Large Facility Size
Big 4 Banks and Private Lenders both scale past $2M, but Private Lenders move faster for a large limit when property security is already in place.

Picking a Line of Credit Against the S.T.A.R. Framework

S
Security
Decide upfront whether you're willing to put up a GSA or property. That single answer eliminates most of the panel before you compare a single rate.
T
Timeframe
How fast do you actually need the limit live? If it's this week, the bank tier is off the table regardless of rate - fintech or specialist non-bank is the real shortlist.
A
Amount
Size the limit against your actual seasonal or cyclical draw pattern, not a round number - an oversized limit just means more line fees on capacity you never touch.
R
Rate
Compare the effective cost of drawn funds, not the headline rate alone - line fees and undrawn-balance charges change the real number more than most borrowers expect.

Real-World Case Study

GOLD COAST BUILDING SUPPLIES

A Gold Coast building and trades supplies wholesaler needed to fund a seasonal stock buy-in ahead of the summer renovation rush - roughly $80,000 of additional inventory, needed on the floor within a week of the supplier's early-order discount closing. Their existing bank relationship offered a business line of credit at 10.8% p.a., but the formal application, security documentation, and valuation process meant a realistic 3-week timeline before funds were drawable - well past the discount window. They instead opened a $80,000 fintech line of credit at 19.5% p.a., approved and drawable in 36 hours against a director guarantee alone. The facility cost roughly $1,560 more in interest over the 8-week stock cycle than the bank option would have - but it captured a $4,200 early-order discount the bank timeline would have missed entirely, a net gain of around $2,640. They've since applied for a second-tier bank facility to run alongside it for the next cycle, once the seasonal timing pressure isn't a factor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a fintech line of credit worse than a bank one?

Not worse - different. A fintech facility costs more in interest and fees, but it's live in days rather than weeks and doesn't require property security. For a time-sensitive need it can be the objectively better choice even at a higher rate; for a facility you'll hold for years with no urgency, a bank line of credit is usually cheaper over time.

Can I have more than one line of credit at once?

Yes. Many businesses run a fintech facility for fast, flexible access alongside a lower-rate bank facility for larger, planned drawdowns. Lenders will factor any existing facility's limit into serviceability (your ability to repay based on cash flow) when assessing a second one, whether it's drawn or not.

What limit can I actually get?

Most panels size an unsecured or GSA-only line of credit against monthly revenue and cash flow trend, commonly landing between 10 to 25% of annual turnover. Property security lifts the ceiling substantially, into the high six or seven figures depending on available equity.

Do I need to redraw the full limit to pay interest?

No. Interest is charged only on the drawn balance, not the approved limit. Some lenders also charge a smaller ongoing line fee or undrawn-balance fee on the unused portion - check this in the fee schedule, since it changes the real cost of holding a limit you don't fully use.

How fast can a line of credit actually be approved?

Fintech lenders commonly approve and fund within 24 to 48 hours based on bank-feed data alone. Specialist non-bank lenders typically take 3 to 7 business days. Bank and property-secured facilities usually take 1 to 4 weeks due to formal credit assessment and, where relevant, a property valuation.

What happens if I don't use my limit?

You generally still owe any ongoing line fee or undrawn-balance charge, but no interest accrues on funds you haven't drawn. An unused limit isn't free to hold, but it's far cheaper than drawn debt - which is why sizing the limit to your actual pattern of use matters more than maximising it.

Can I upgrade from a fintech LOC to a bank LOC later?

Yes, and it's a common sequence - use a fintech facility to solve an immediate timing problem, then apply for a lower-rate bank facility once there's no urgency and you have 6 to 12 months of trading history through the fintech facility to show consistent, controlled usage.

Does comparing line of credit lenders affect my credit score?

Comparing indicative rates through a broker or comparison service typically uses a soft credit check, with no credit score impact. Only a formal application triggers a hard inquiry - comparing the panel before applying protects your file from multiple hard inquiries.
