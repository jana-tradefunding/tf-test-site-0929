Frequently asked questions

Is the Compare Report really free?

Yes. There is no cost to generate or receive your Compare Report, and no obligation to proceed with any of the matched lenders.

Will this affect my credit file?

No. Matching your profile against our lender panel uses a soft credit check only, so there is no credit score impact and no credit footprint.

How long does the report take?

Most reports are delivered within 24 hours of submitting your details.
