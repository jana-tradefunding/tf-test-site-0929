Frequently asked questions.

What are the minimum criteria to qualify?

Most lenders require at least 6 months trading history and $10,000+ in average monthly revenue. Some non-bank lenders will consider newer businesses on a case-by-case basis.

Do I need to put up my house as security?

No. Unsecured facilities are available up to $500,000. Larger facilities can be secured against business assets or a director guarantee, without requiring a residential property mortgage.

How long does funding take?

Pre-qualification typically takes 24 hours. Once you accept an offer and complete the lender’s formal application, funds typically land within 3 to 5 business days.

Are there discounts for early payout?

Some lenders offer reduced interest for early payout, while others charge a fixed-fee structure regardless of payout timing. Your Compare Report shows the payout terms for each matched lender.
